# YourScene — Facebook Group Intro Posts (value-first, not spam)

> **Golden rule:** Most soccer/WC groups ban self-promo. Lead with a genuine question or value.
> Drop the YourScene link only after you've contributed and people are engaging — or if a group
> explicitly allows it. When in doubt, mention the app in a reply, not the original post.
> **Workflow:** join → like a few posts → comment on 2-3 → THEN post your intro.

---

## A. NATIONAL WC GROUPS (FIFA World Cup 2026, World Cup USA 2026)
*Big, broad, less local. Post a question that travels.*

**Post 1 (question):**
> Genuine question for everyone traveling or local: how are you deciding WHERE to watch each match? Sticking to one bar all tournament, or chasing the best crowd for each game? 🌍⚽

**Post 2 (city pride):**
> Which US host city is going to have the BEST atmosphere — NYC, Miami, Dallas, or LA? I'll go first: NYC, and it's not close. Convince me otherwise 👇

## Posting cadence (from playbook: 3 FB posts/day)
- Pick 3 groups/day, rotate A→E so you're not repetitive.
- Always reply to commenters — that's where the real connections (and signups) happen.
- Only the **soft app mention** carries the link, and only in replies or link-friendly groups.
- Log each post in `04-tracker.csv` (Type = Community).
