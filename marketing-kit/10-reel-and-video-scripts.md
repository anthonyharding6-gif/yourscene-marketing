# YourScene — Video Scripts (10 Reels + founder intro + product demo + venue spotlight)

> Covers the deck's full video requirement. Each reel: 15-30s, vertical 9:16. Batch-film 4-5 at once.
> Hook in the first 2 seconds or people scroll. End every one with: "Find your scene → link in bio."

---

## REEL 1 — Founder intro (FILM FIRST)
- **Shot:** You, talking to camera, somewhere with city/soccer energy (a bar, a street).
- **Hook (0-2s):** "The World Cup is in New York and I had one problem."
- **Body:** "You can't tell which spot's gonna be electric until you're already standing in it. So I built YourScene — it shows you the real-time energy of every watch party and venue near you."
- **CTA:** "Feel the crowd. Find your scene. Link in bio."
- **Caption:** Built this because I was tired of guessing where the vibe was. World Cup's coming — let's find the best crowds in the city together ⚽ #WorldCup2026 #NYC #soccer
- **On-screen text:** "POV: you built an app so you'd never walk into a dead bar again"

## REEL 2 — Product demo / energy map (FILM ONCE APP IS LIVE)

## VENUE SPOTLIGHT template (reusable as venues confirm — fills Reels 5-7 + posts)
- **Format:** 1 venue, 15s. Their best feature + a match they're showing + their @handle tagged.
- **Script:** "If you're watching [match] in [neighborhood], go to [venue] — [one standout detail]. They're on YourScene." (Tag them so they reshare → free reach.)
- **Caption:** Featured spot for the World Cup: @[venue]. Find the full map on YourScene. ⚽

## PRODUCT DEMO video (longer, for site/pinned post)
- **Length:** 45-60s. Screen-record + voiceover.
- **Beat 1:** The problem — "Where do I watch?" (5s)
- **Beat 2:** Open YourScene → the energy map, green pulses (10s)
- **Beat 3:** Tap a venue → crowd score, who's there, events (15s)
- **Beat 4:** Fan Passport — your interests, your tribes (10s)
- **Beat 5:** "Feel the crowd. Find your scene. Live for World Cup 2026." (5s)

## Filming tips (you're part-time — keep it cheap)
- Phone only. Natural light. Film 4-5 reels in one session to batch.
- Hooks first 2 seconds. Captions on-screen (80% watch muted).
- Repurpose each Reel → IG Reel + TikTok + X video + YouTube Short.
- Tag every venue you feature so they reshare = free distribution.
