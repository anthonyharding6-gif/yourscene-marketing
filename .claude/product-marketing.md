## Product
**YourScene** — a mobile app (iOS & Android) that shows the **real-time crowd energy** of live events, nightlife venues, and watch parties around you. Core features: live crowd-density scores, a neon energy-pulse map, friends/groups tied to venues, and a "Fan Passport" (interests, tribes, privacy controls).
**Site/waitlist:** https://yourscene.netlify.app/
**Launch:** June 11, 2026 — launching first for **World Cup 2026** (NYC is a host region; MetLife hosts the final).

## Category & positioning
- **Category claim:** "Real-time crowd energy" — a new layer on top of events/nightlife discovery. Not a listings directory, not a ticketing app — a *live vibe map*.
- **Positioning statement:** For event-goers and sports fans who hate guessing where the night actually is, YourScene shows the real-time energy of every venue near you — so you always find the crowd, not an empty room. Unlike Yelp/Google (static reviews) or Eventbrite (tickets), YourScene is live.
- **Tagline:** *Feel the crowd. Find your scene.*

## Audience (two-sided)
**Demand side — Fans (18-40):** sports fans, nightlife-goers, music/food lovers, social connectors. WC2026 beachhead = soccer fans in NYC who want to find the best watch party for each match and their country's crowd.
- Pains: "Which spot will actually be packed?" · FOMO · arriving somewhere dead · scattered info across IG/Google/group chats.
- Jobs: find where my people / my country are watching; feel the city's energy; not waste a night out.

**Supply side — Venues/promoters:** sports bars, rooftops, breweries, restaurants. WC2026 beachhead = NYC soccer bars (20 mapped in `marketing-kit/05`).
- Pains: empty mid-week, hard to reach fans, compete for foot traffic during the tournament.
- Jobs: fill seats, get discovered, show off their energy. YourScene value: verification, event promotion, targeted fan matching, free featured listing.

## Messaging pillars (use across all copy)
1. **Live, not static** — "Green means it's buzzing." Real-time beats reviews.
2. **Find your people** — country crowds, tribes, friends at venues (Fan Passport).
3. **Never waste a night** — kill FOMO and the dead-bar gamble.
4. **Built for the World Cup** — the city's about to be electric; be where it is.

Energetic, in-the-know local, FOMO-aware, a little playful. Short punchy lines. Emojis ok (⚽🟢🔥). Never corporate. Talks like a friend who always knows where the party is.
