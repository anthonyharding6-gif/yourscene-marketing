## Goal & voice
Goal pre-launch: waitlist signups + buzz; at launch: installs. Action: tap the link / install.
Voice (from `.claude/product-marketing.md`): energetic local friend, FOMO-aware, short, playful, neon-green energy. "Feel the crowd. Find your scene."

## Hook formulas (first 1 second decides everything)
- **Contrarian:** "Unpopular opinion: the crowd matters more than the match."
- **Value:** "5 NYC bars that go feral for the World Cup. Save this."
- **Curiosity:** "The most diverse soccer neighborhood in NYC isn't where you think."
- **Story:** "We've all walked into a dead bar on a big match night…"
- **FOMO/Q:** "Where's the World Cup energy actually going to be in NYC?"

## THE REPURPOSING ENGINE (your biggest time-saver)
You're part-time — never create from scratch. Extract "content atoms" from a few pillar pieces:

**The flagship article (`outputs/content/where-to-watch-world-cup-nyc.md`) → 15+ posts:**
- 20 venue **spotlight** posts (one per venue, use the Canva template)
- 4 neighborhood **carousels** (Midtown / Williamsburg / Astoria / Downtown)
- 1 X/Threads **thread** ("Best WC bars in NYC, ranked 🧵")
- 1 IG **Reel** ("5 bars that go feral — save this") → TikTok + Shorts
- Country posts ("Where Argentina watches in NYC")

**Each Reel → 4 posts:** IG Reel + TikTok + YouTube Short + X video. Film once, post four places.

**Each match day → real content:** a 10-sec clip of a buzzing venue = the highest-converting proof you can post ("this is the scene right now 🟢").

Workflow: 1 pillar piece → extract 5-10 atoms → adapt per platform → schedule across the week → reshare evergreen in 3-6 months.

## Short-form video upgrades (apply to `marketing-kit/10` reels)
**The 3-second rule** — every Reel needs all three in second one: VISUAL hook + VERBAL hook + TEXT overlay.
**Structures:**
- *Problem-solution* (Reel 1, 8): hook the dead-bar problem → agitate → YourScene → CTA.
- *List* (Reel 6): "5 bars that go feral" → one every 5-8s → CTA.
- *Tutorial/demo* (Reel 2): show the lit-up map FIRST → "here's how" → CTA.
**Captions:** always on (watched muted), max 2 lines / 3-5 words, bold sans-serif + outline, highlight key word in green, timed to speech. Tool: CapCut (free).
**Avoid:** slow hooks, no text overlay, bad audio, too long, no CTA.

## Daily engagement routine (30 min — this IS the growth)
1. Reply to every comment/DM on your posts (5)
2. Comment with real value on 5-10 posts from NYC soccer/nightlife accounts + the warm creators (15)
3. Reshare a venue's or fan's post with a line of credit (5)
4. DM 2-3 new engaged followers (5)
Engagement in the first hour after posting drives reach — be present right after you post.

## Reverse-engineer what works
Follow the warm creators (Matty, Kevin, SportsGirlNYC, PharaohTalks) + top WC accounts. Note their best hooks/formats; layer your voice. Don't guess — copy what's already winning in your niche.

## Metrics that matter (weekly review)
- Reels: **saves + shares > likes** (the algorithm rewards them) + watch-through
- Comments (higher value than likes), profile visits → **link clicks to waitlist** (UTM via `outputs/10`)
- Follower growth rate; best posting times from your data
- Weekly: top 3 posts (why?), bottom 3 (learn), double down on the winning format
