# YourScene — Cold Email Sequences

> Applies the `cold-email` skill. Upgrades the single-message outreach in `marketing-kit/01` into
> 5-touch sequences that actually get replies (55% of replies come from follow-ups). Peer voice,
> ruthlessly short, one low-friction ask, a NEW angle every touch. Use email where you have it;
> the same copy adapts to IG DM (shorten further). Last updated: June 3, 2026.

## Rules baked into every email below
- **Subject:** 2-4 words, lowercase, looks internal (no product pitch, no first name, no emoji).
- **Lead with their world** ("you/your" > "I/we"). Cut any sentence that doesn't move them to reply.
- **One ask**, interest-based ("want in?" not "got 30 min?").
- **Personalization must connect to the problem** — if you can delete the first line and the email still makes sense, it's not working.
- No AI tells: never "I hope this finds you well," "I came across," "leverage," "synergy."

## Cadence (per the data)
| Touch | Day | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Initial | 0 | personalized hook + core value + soft ask |
| 2 — Follow-up | 3 | new value (the free SEO list exposure) |
| 3 — Follow-up | 7 | social proof + loss aversion |
| 4 — Follow-up | 14 | a useful insight (morning-kickoff gap) |
| 5 — Breakup | 21 | 1-2-3 close, then stop |
Send **Tue-Thu, 9-11am or 1-3pm**. Honor the breakup — if you send it, don't email again.

# SEQUENCE A — Venues
Value prop: YourScene's live energy map sends fans to your bar; free to be listed. Problem it solves: filling *every* match (esp. random morning group-stage games), and being findable on match day.

**Touch 1 — subject: `world cup crowd`**
> Hey [Name] — [personal hook tied to them, e.g. "saw [venue] is showing every match on the 50+ screens"]. The tricky part during the tournament isn't the England game — it's filling the random 9am group-stage ones.
>
> We're launching YourScene before kickoff: a live map that shows fans which spots are actually buzzing right now and sends them there. Free to be on it.
>
> Want [venue] on the map for June 11?

**Touch 2 — Day 3 — subject: `quick one`**
> Hey [Name] — following up with something useful either way: we're publishing a guide to NYC's best World Cup bars by neighborhood, and [venue] belongs on the [neighborhood] list. Want me to include you (and add you to the live map)? Takes 2 minutes.

**Touch 3 — Day 7 — subject: `who's in`**
> Hey [Name] — a few [neighborhood] spots are already locked in for the opener. Don't want [venue] to be the one fans can't find on match day. Want me to add you?

**Touch 4 — Day 14 — subject: `the 9am problem`**
> Hey [Name] — opener's June 11. One thing most bars miss: morning kickoffs are where the empty seats are — fans don't know who's open at 9am. Being on the live map fixes exactly that. Add [venue]?

**Touch 5 — Day 21 — subject: `last one`**
> Haven't heard back, so I'll leave it here. Reply with a number:
> 1 — yes, add [venue] to the map
> 2 — not now, check back after the group stage
> 3 — not interested
> Either way, good luck with the tournament. ⚽

# SEQUENCE B — Influencers / creators
Value prop: featured-creator spot, early access for their audience, co-promo. Problem: they need fresh WC content + something to give their followers.

**Touch 1 — subject: `world cup collab`**
> Hey [Name] — [personal: a specific post of theirs, e.g. "your Times Square fan interviews are exactly the WC energy"]. We're launching YourScene before kickoff — a live map of where the crowd actually is for every match. Feels made for your audience. Open to a collab — featured creator, early access for your followers, or co-promoting the best watch parties?

**Touch 2 — Day 3 — subject: `idea`**
> Hey [Name] — one concrete idea: we give your followers early access + a "find your country's crowd" feature for the opener, you get first content on the energy map before anyone. Want me to send a preview?

**Touch 3 — Day 7 — subject: `who's on board`**
> Hey [Name] — lining up a few NYC creators for launch week. Would love you in the first group given your soccer audience. Worth a quick look?

**Touch 4 — Day 14 — subject: `kickoff`**
> Hey [Name] — opener's June 11 and the city's about to be electric. If you want a fresh angle for match-day content, the live energy map is yours to play with early. In?

**Touch 5 — Day 21 — subject: `last one`**
> No worries if it's not your thing. Reply 1 (let's do it) / 2 (after the group stage) / 3 (not for me). Either way, enjoy the tournament.

# SEQUENCE C — Communities / supporters clubs / partnerships
Value prop: a findable home base for their members during matches; connect their community to the right watch events. Problem: members scatter; hard to coordinate.

**Touch 1 — subject: `[country] fans`**
> Hi [Name] — with [country] in the World Cup, your members are about to be looking for where to gather for each match. We're launching YourScene, a live map of where the [country] crowd is watching across NYC. We'd feature your events and point fans to you — no cost. Worth a quick chat?

**Touch 2 — Day 3 — subject: `home base`**
> Hi [Name] — concretely: we can make [org] the home base [country] fans find on the map for every match, and surface fans searching for your community. Want me to set it up before June 11?

**Touch 3 — Day 7 — subject: `before kickoff`**
> Hi [Name] — locking in launch partners this week. Would love [org] to be the [country] anchor. Two minutes to get you in — interested?

**Touch 4 — Day 14 — subject: `the opener`**
> Hi [Name] — kickoff's June 11. If members ask "where's everyone watching?", being on the map answers it instantly. Happy to feature your gatherings — want in?

**Touch 5 — Day 21 — subject: `last one`**
> I'll leave it here. Reply 1 (yes, feature us) / 2 (reach out later) / 3 (no thanks). Either way — good luck to [country] this summer.

## Working the sequences (part-time friendly)
- Log every send + which touch in `marketing-kit/04-tracker.csv` (private). Reply → stop the sequence.
- Personalize Touch 1 always (the hook). Touches 2-5 can run near-as-written.
- IG DM version: send Touch 1 + Touch 3 + Touch 5 only (DMs fatigue faster than inboxes).
- Reply target: a few short words ("want in?") — make saying yes a one-liner.
