# YourScene — Launch Plan (ORB framework)

> Applies the `launch` skill. Launch = June 11, 2026 (World Cup kickoff / Mexico opener).
> Philosophy: a launch isn't one moment — it's momentum across Owned, Rented, Borrowed channels,
> all funneling back to owned (waitlist → app installs → push).

## The moment
**Anchor the launch to the Mexico vs South Africa opener (Jun 11).** The whole city is already paying attention that day — ride it instead of competing with it. "The World Cup is live in NYC, and so is YourScene."

## ORB channel plan

### OWNED (you control — funnel everything here)
- **Waitlist site** (optimize first — `outputs/03`). Add a real "Where to watch in NYC" page for SEO + value.
- **Email/push to waitlist:** pre-launch teaser (Jun 9), launch-day blast (Jun 11 AM), match-day pushes throughout.
- **Your IG/X/Threads** — countdown already scheduled in `marketing-kit/06`.

### RENTED (platforms, algorithm-dependent)
- IG Reels + TikTok + Shorts (scripts in `marketing-kit/10`) — post the founder intro + energy-map demo.
- Threads/X question-bait daily (`marketing-kit/02`).
- Reddit/Discord/FB value-first (`marketing-kit/07-09`).
- **Product Hunt:** optional — launch a few days *after* (Jun 13-14) once you have real activity to show; PH rewards live products, not empty maps.

### BORROWED (other people's audiences — highest near-term leverage)
- **Venues reshare** your spotlight Reels (tag them → they share to their followers). 20 targets ready.
- **Influencers** (Matty 280K, Kevin 143K, SportsGirl, PharaohTalks, Ali ~47K) — collab/reshare on launch day.
- **@harbornewyorkcity (55K)** — ask them to reshare the launch (warm).
- National-team communities (St. George's, Ironbound, American Outlaws) post to their members.
