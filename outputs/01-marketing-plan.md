# YourScene — Marketing Plan (AARRR)

> Applies the `marketing-plan` skill. Structured by Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Referral → Revenue.
> Stage: pre-launch, two-sided consumer app, part-time solo operator, ~$0 paid budget. Launch Jun 11, 2026.

## 1. Executive summary
**3 big bets:**
1. **Supply-seed the beachhead** — launch with 25-50 real NYC venues + the 6 Harbor WC events so the map is never empty (the #1 failure mode for a crowd-energy app).
2. **Ride the World Cup wave** — use the tournament as the wedge: every match is a reason to open the app.
3. **Owned-channel compounding** — convert all hype into waitlist → push notifications → DAU.

**90-day priority:** get to a living map in NYC with real daily activity, 250+ waitlist, 25-50 venues.
**12-month outcome:** the default "where's the energy tonight" app for NYC nightlife/sports, ready to expand to Miami/LA.

## 2. Strategic frame
Category: real-time crowd energy. ICP: NYC fans 18-40 + NYC sports-bar/rooftop venues. Model: consumer app, two-sided marketplace dynamics (need supply before demand). Voice: energetic local friend. See `.claude/product-marketing.md`.

## 3. Current state (honest)
- Team: 1 part-time growth (Anthony). Budget: ~$0 paid. Channels live: IG, X/Threads, waitlist site.
- Done: 13-doc launch kit, 20 venue targets, 5 warm emails drafted, week of posts scheduled.
- Stuck/risk: app not yet demoable (no screen-record for Reel 2); single operator bandwidth.

## 4. ACQUISITION — strangers → aware
**Now (pre-launch, organic):**
- Venue outreach (IG DM, 20 mapped) → venues reshare to their followers = borrowed reach. `marketing-kit/05`
- Warm influencers (Matty 280K, Kevin 143K, SportsGirl, PharaohTalks, Ali) → collabs/reshares. `cold-email`+`co-marketing`
- Community: FB groups, Reddit, Discord (value-first). `marketing-kit/07-09`
- Social question-bait on Threads/X + Reels. `social`, `video`
- SEO: a "Where to watch the World Cup in NYC" page on the site (huge search volume right now). `ai-seo`, `programmatic-seo`
**12-month:** programmatic neighborhood/venue pages, ASO, referrals engine, repeatable launch cadence.

## 5. ACTIVATION — aware → first real experience
- Waitlist site must convert (see `outputs/03-landing-cro-and-copy.md`). `cro`, `signup`
- App onboarding: pick country + tribes + location → instantly show a live/near map (avoid empty-state). `onboarding`
- ASO listing optimized before launch (`outputs/04-aso-app-store-listing.md`). `aso`
- Launch-day push: "Mexico kicks off — here's where it's buzzing." `emails`/push

## 6. RETENTION — keep them
- Every match = a re-engagement trigger (push: "Argentina in 1hr, 3 spots near you lighting up").
- Daily energy map is inherently habit-forming during the tournament window — capitalize with notifications.
- Post-WC: extend to nightlife/concerts so the habit survives July+. `churn-prevention`

## 7. REFERRAL — they bring more
- Inherently social: "find your friends at venues" = invite loop. Seed it. `referrals`
- "Invite 3 friends, unlock your country's crowd map early" pre-launch mechanic. See `outputs/05`.
- Venue + influencer reshares = the strongest near-term referral. `community-marketing`

## 8. REVENUE (later — not the focus pre-launch)
- Free for fans. Venue monetization later: featured placement, verified profiles, promoted events, fan-match leads. `pricing`, `paywalls`
- Don't gate growth now — get the network first.

## 9. 90-day roadmap
- **Wk 1-2 (Unblock, → Jun 11):** send all outreach, seed venues, launch. Get app demo recorded.
- **Wk 3-4 (Foundation):** turn WC traffic into retained DAU; ship "where to watch" SEO page; ASO live.
- **Wk 5-8 (Velocity):** referral loop on; venue self-serve onboarding; double down on best channel.
- **Wk 9-12 (Compound):** post-WC retention pivot to nightlife; plan Miami/LA; first paid tests if data supports.

## 11. The single most important thing
**Supply before demand.** A crowd-energy app that opens to an empty map dies. Every pre-launch hour goes to venues + seeding real activity first.
