# YourScene — Referrals + Community Engine

> Applies `referrals` + `community-marketing`. YourScene is inherently social (find friends at venues),
> so referral is a growth *mechanic*, not an afterthought. Build the loop in from day one.

## Why referral matters most here
A crowd-energy app gets better with every user (more presence = more accurate energy). That's a network effect — engineer the invite loop so growth compounds.

## Pre-launch referral mechanic (waitlist)
- **Skip-the-line:** "Invite 3 friends → unlock your country's crowd map early."
- Give each signup a unique share link (post-signup screen — see `outputs/03`).
- Leaderboard optional: top referrers get "Founding Fan" Passport badge.

## In-app referral loops (at launch)
1. **Find-your-friends invite:** "Your friends aren't on the map yet — invite them to see who's out." (core loop)
2. **Group/plan share:** picking a venue → "Share this spot" → friends get a deep link into the live map.
3. **Founding Fan badge:** early users get status; status is shareable = built-in WOM.

## Community marketing (build a tribe, don't just broadcast)
- **Owned community:** a YourScene Discord or Close Friends list of "Founding Fans" — early access, polls, they shape the app. Turns first users into evangelists.
- **Borrowed communities:** participate genuinely in NYCFC/American Outlaws/r/soccer/FB groups (playbooks in `marketing-kit/07-09`). Be the helpful "where's the energy" person.
- **Ambassadors:** recruit 5-10 hyper-connected fans (one per country/neighborhood) to seed energy + invite their crews. Reward with Founding Fan status + swag.

## Venue + influencer as referral channels (highest near-term ROI)
- Every featured venue that reshares = a referral to their whole following. Make reshares effortless (pre-made spotlight Reels, tag them).
- Influencer codes/links → track which creator drives signups (`analytics`).

## 30-day referral checklist
- [ ] Post-signup share link + skip-the-line copy live (`outputs/03`)
- [ ] In-app "invite friends" loop at launch
- [ ] Founding Fan badge/status defined
- [ ] Recruit 5-10 ambassadors (country/neighborhood)
- [ ] Stand up Founding Fans community (Discord or IG Close Friends)
- [ ] UTM/codes so every referral source is tracked (`analytics`)

## Related skills to run next
`analytics` (track referral sources), `emails` (referral nudge sequence), `co-marketing` (joint pushes with venues/creators).
