# Where to Watch the World Cup in NYC (2026): 20 Best Bars & Watch Parties

**Last updated: June 3, 2026 · By the YourScene team (NYC)**

**Quick answer:** The best places to watch the 2026 World Cup in New York City are dedicated soccer bars like Football Factory at Legends (Midtown), Smithfield Hall (Flatiron), and Banter (Williamsburg), plus national-team hubs like Boca Juniors Steakhouse (Argentina, Queens). Most open early for the 8 AM and noon kickoffs, show every match, and fill fast for big games — arrive 60-90 minutes early.

The World Cup runs June 11–July 19, 2026, with NYC/New Jersey hosting matches and the final at MetLife Stadium. Below are the 20 best spots to catch the action, by borough, with the neighborhoods and national-team crowds each is known for.

## FAQ

**Where is the best place to watch the World Cup in NYC?**
Football Factory at Legends in Midtown is widely considered NYC's top soccer bar, home to 30+ supporter clubs and dozens of screens. For a specific national team, go to that team's hub — e.g. Boca Juniors Steakhouse for Argentina, Rivercrest for the USA.

**What time do World Cup matches start in New York?**
Most 2026 group-stage matches kick off in the morning or early afternoon ET (commonly 8 AM, noon, or 3 PM). Many NYC bars open early specifically for the 8 AM games.

**Do I need a reservation to watch the World Cup at a NYC bar?**
For big matches, yes — reserve where possible (e.g. Jones Wood Foundry on Resy, Eastpoint for groups). Walk-in spots like The Red Lion fill fast, so arrive early.
