## High-value campaigns (specific, 2-week-launchable)

**1. Harbor = "Official Launch Partner" ★ do first**
- Harbor reshares the YourScene launch to its 55K; you make Harbor's 6 WC events your flagship featured listings + run a "Harbor takeover" Story on launch day.
- Why it wins: warm, zero cost, solves your seed-supply problem and their fill-seats problem in one move.

**2. Creator "Best WC Bars in NYC" collab**
- A creator films a venue-tour Reel *powered by YourScene*; you feature them as a Founding Creator; they post to their followers with your link (UTM per creator, `outputs/data/utm-builder.csv`).
- Offer: featured spot + early access for their community + first look at the energy map.

**3. Supporter-community "home base" partnership**
- Make each club (American Outlaws Queens → Rivercrest, St. George's → England spots) the anchor their members find on the map. They post it to members; you surface their gatherings.

**4. Joint giveaway (list-building)**
- With a venue or beer brand: "Win the ultimate World Cup watch party for you + 5 friends." Entry = join the waitlist + tag friends. Co-promoted by both. Cheap, fast, viral-ish.

**5. Media segment**
- Pitch Telemundo/Univision a "where NYC's Mexico/Spain crowds are watching" segment featuring the live energy map. Pitch Gothamist/amNY/worldcup.nyc the "Where to Watch in NYC" guide (`outputs/content/`) — gets you listed where AI engines already cite (`outputs/06`).

## Approach (use `cold-email` Sequence B/C)
Lead with *their* win, propose one concrete idea, one ask. Bring: your angle, the specific campaign, what you'll provide (listings/exposure), what you want (reshare/feature). For warm ones (Harbor, the creators) — just reconnect and pitch the specific campaign.

## Simple terms (keep it a one-paragraph DM, not a contract)
Align on: who promotes what + when, who makes the asset (you make the share kit — pre-made spotlight Reel + caption + link), how you'll both measure (UTM links + reshare reach). No cash, no exclusivity — pure cross-promo before June 11.

## Metrics
Per partner: signups/installs from their UTM link, reshare reach, new followers, and whether they'd do it again. Track in the tracker; the UTM table (`outputs/data/utm-builder.csv`) already has per-creator sources.
